About GTL AI

GTL AI was founded to close the gap between what AI can do in a lab and what it can do for real businesses and people. Our founding team brings together experience in operations, finance, data, and engineering — united by a shared conviction that the next wave of AI products has to be trustworthy, useful, and built to last.
